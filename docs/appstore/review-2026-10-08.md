## 2. Cevap metni (App Store Connect › App Review › Reply)

```text

1. Screen recording
Recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS with the submitted build (1.0.0, build 22). It starts with launching the app and shows onboarding, account registration with email and sign-in (including the verification e-mail), the typical learning flow (daily round, a Path speaking step with the speech recognition permission and the Report sheet, a writing task in Skills with its Report option, walk mode), the user content reporting and blocking tools, the Premium purchase screen with the title, length and price of each subscription and the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links, Restore, sign-out and sign-in with email, and deletion of the newly registered account. The App Store purchase sheet is not in the recording (the device asked for its Apple Account password); purchases can be tested with apple-review-free@lernomi.app (see 3).

2. Purpose and audience
Lernomi is a language learning app for German and English, from beginner (A1) to advanced (C1). It is mainly for Turkish speakers who need German for study, work or family reunification in German-speaking countries; the interface is also available in English and German. The problem it solves: learners can read and memorise words, but rarely get to speak and write with feedback or see where they stand before an exam. Lernomi combines a daily vocabulary round with spaced repetition, a Path of units (reading, listening, speaking with an AI conversation character, writing with AI feedback, grammar and quizzes), practice exams in four skills, and a hands-free walk mode. The app is rated 18+.

3. Setup and access
- Review account with Premium: apple-review@lernomi.app / [[REVIEW_PASSWORD]]
- Review account without Premium (for the purchase flow): apple-review-free@lernomi.app / [[REVIEW_PASSWORD]]
Both accounts do not expire and have no two-factor authentication. Sign in with "Continue with email". Both skip our bot check: if a "Verify you are human" box appears, ignore it and tap "Sign in". Onboarding: course German, level "From scratch", goal "Easy". The app also works without an account ("Continue without an account"). Main areas: the Learn tab (daily round, practice, weekly quiz, mock exams, walk mode), Path, Skills and Community; Profile is the avatar at the top, Settings is the gear icon in Profile. No sample files are needed.

4. External services
- Cloudflare: network and bot protection (Turnstile), Workers AI (language model and speech recognition), encrypted off-site backups (R2)
- Groq and Deepgram: language model and speech recognition
- Microsoft Azure Speech: speech recognition in walk mode, speech synthesis
- Microsoft Edge read-aloud service: speech synthesis
- Apple In-App Purchase and RevenueCat: subscription payments and subscription state
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In: authentication
- Firebase Cloud Messaging: push notifications
- Resend: account e-mails (verification, password reset)
- Netcup: servers in the EU
Text is sent to AI providers only after the user allows it on the consent screen; audio is not stored. The same list is in section 6 of our privacy policy: https://www.lernomi.app/privacy/en

5. Regional differences
The app works the same in all regions: the same features and the same content. Only the subscription price (set per App Store storefront) and the interface language (Turkish, English or German) differ.

6. Regulated industry and third-party material
Lernomi does not operate in a regulated industry. All learning content, exercises and practice exams are written by Lernomi; they are not official exam material, Lernomi is not affiliated with any exam provider, and the certificates of achievement in the app are not official certificates. The character voices were designed with an open-source speech model (Apache 2.0) from written descriptions; they are not real people's voices.

7. In-App Purchase
Lernomi Premium, an auto-renewable subscription, monthly or yearly, with a one-month free trial for new subscribers. Premium unlocks every AI speaking and writing task in Path and Skills right away (free users get 4 speaking and 4 writing tasks per level), all practice exams (free users get 1 per level) and walk mode with the screen off. Daily fair-use limits are shown on the purchase screen. To reach it: Profile (avatar at the top) › "Go Premium", or tap any locked item, such as a locked practice exam. The purchase screen shows each subscription's title, length and price, the trial, the renewal terms, Restore, and links to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. "Manage subscription" opens Apple's subscription management sheet. To test, sign in with apple-review-free@lernomi.app; purchases in review run in the sandbox.
```
